# Nugget

An open-source (Apache-2.0), local-first, cross-platform personal AI. It learns your work continuously, drafts in your
voice, prepares work for your approval, and operates your computer, with every step inspectable and owned by you.

Status: **planning**. See [docs/PLAN.md](docs/PLAN.md) and the competitive research in [docs/research/](docs/research/).
