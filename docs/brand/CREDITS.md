# Image credits

Every image Heed ships is original or in the public domain. Nothing is copied from the moodboards.

## Public-domain paintings (CC0)

From The Metropolitan Museum of Art's Open Access collection, released under
[Creative Commons Zero (CC0)](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Each one is
duotoned to Heed ultramarine on cream with `docs/brand/tools/duotone.py`, and stored in
`apps/desktop/src/brand/img/` at no more than 250 KB.

## Original procedural imagery (Apache-2.0)

Generated by `docs/brand/tools/procedural.py` from seeded noise, so it can be regenerated:

- `dither-landscape.png`: a two-colour Bayer-dithered mountain landscape in ultramarine on cream.
- `engraving-landscape.png`: the same kind of landscape as line engraving in signal deep.
- `sky.webp`: a generated daylight sky (an ultramarine to cream gradient with noise clouds).

The logo, mark and app icon in `docs/brand/assets/` are original artwork (Apache-2.0), drawn from
Fraunces outlines (SIL Open Font License).
