The first question on first run, on the welcome backdrop: two equal, calm cards. Choosing one moves on. Nothing is preselected, and the fork never comes back as a nag.

How do you want to run Heed?

You can switch later in Settings. Your files and settings come with you.

  • .ob-fork holds two button.ob-fork-cards. Set aria-pressed="true" (or .is-selected) for the outline if the choice is kept on screen.
  • Card heading: Fraunces at 28px. The kicker is a 12px label, the ticks are ultramarine, and the price line sits at the bottom so both cards end together.
  • Under 640px the cards stack. Reduced motion drops the 2px hover lift.

Choose your AI

Heed sends it only the passages a question needs.

  • .ai-choices holds label.ai-choices, each with a visually hidden radio, .ai-choice-icon, a title and blurb, and a right-hand .ai-choice-status. The checked row gets a 2px ultramarine outline; .ai-choice-body opens under it.
  • For the key, set .key-field[data-state] to checking (muted, "Checking…"), valid (green, says what it found) or invalid (red, says why and what to try next). The key is shown masked in mono.
  • ChatGPT sign-in: while the browser is open, the status reads "Waiting for your browser…" with a spinner. A device code, if one is used, goes in the body in Geist Mono at 20px.
