# Lemon drizzle cake

Ingredients: 225g butter, 225g caster sugar, 4 eggs, 275g self-raising flour, zest of 2 lemons.
Drizzle: juice of 1.5 lemons, 85g sugar. 45 to 50 minutes at 180C.
