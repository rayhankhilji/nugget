# GG4 / A3: card actions and dry-run diffs

Scenario: `qa/GG4/scenarios/actions.py` (deterministic; `--out DIR`; `--runtime --chat-model --embed-model --models-home` for CI with a real model; `--only exec,binding,ttl,nomodel,tz`).
New harness piece: `qa/GG4/harness/fake_llm.py`, a tiny OpenAI-compatible chat server. Without `--runtime` it is wired in as a `[[providers]]` entry (`openai_compatible`, tiers triage/light/synthesis, judge off). Every reply carries the marker `FAKE-LLM-7731`.

Corpus: synthetic, written by the scenario and **not** the persona. It contains:
- Lena's unanswered client question, which gives an `unanswered_thread` card
- Maya's dated promise to Owen ("by Friday"), which gives a `follow_up` card
- Maya's question to Iris that nobody answered, which gives a `follow_up` (awaiting reply) card
- an Inkwell renewal notice plus its receipt, which give a `renewal` card
- a dated to-do in `Documents/todo.md`, which gives a `commitment` card
- an external meeting `.ics`, expected to give a `meeting_prep` card (it does not; see N1)

Each phase runs one or more daemons under libfaketime. Default sim time is Thu 2026-10-29 09:00 America/New_York. The TZ phase also runs Wed 2026-11-04 09:00 Europe/London.

Clock jumps: `FtDaemon` sets `FAKETIME_TIMESTAMP_FILE` with `FAKETIME_NO_CACHE=1`, so the clock can jump while the daemon runs. This is how the TTL and `reminder.due` checks work. One quirk: libfaketime reads "@T" from the file as T plus the process's uptime, so `jump()` subtracts the uptime.

Runs: two full runs (r2, r4: 59 checks, about 5 min each) plus one TTL-only rerun after fixing that uptime quirk. Results were identical across runs; nothing here is flaky. Evidence is in `qa/GG4/out/a3/`: `summary.json`, `exec-exercise.json` (every preview next to its effect), `binding.json`, `ttl.json`, `tz.json`, `exec-v0-audit.json` (policy log), `fake_llm.jsonl` (what the daemon sent to the model), `focus-event-NY.ics` and `run.log`.

Repro for everything below, from the repo root:
```
python3 qa/GG4/scenarios/actions.py --out /tmp/a3        # ~5 min, needs target/debug/nuggetd + libfaketime
python3 qa/GG4/scenarios/actions.py --out /tmp/a3 --only exec,binding   # ~30 s
```

## FINDINGS

### A3-1. Reminders and deadlines after a DST change are an hour off (renewal reminder at 08:00 instead of 09:00)
- **Severity:** Medium
- **Repro:** `python3 qa/GG4/scenarios/actions.py --out /tmp/a3 --only tz`. Sim time is Thu 2026-10-29 09:00 America/New_York. The renewal notice says "renews on November 3, 2026". US DST ends Sun Nov 1.
- **Expected:** a reminder at 09:00 local on Nov 2. That is the generator's intent: `Time::from_hms(9,0,0)` the day before.
- **Actual:**
  - Preview: `Reminder at 2026-11-02T09:00:00-04:00: Renewal: Inkwell Studio — $96.00`. On Nov 2 New York is at -05:00, so this instant is **08:00 EST**.
  - `reminder.due` arrived at sim `2026-11-02T08:00:29-05:00` (`ttl.json`, `summary.json` → `due:reminder_fires_on_time.more`).
  - The card label `Remind me 2026-11-02T09:00:00-04:00` shows an offset that is never valid on that date.
- **Frequency:** 2/2 runs (deterministic). London has no DST change in its window, so it passes 1/1.
- **Root cause:** every future wall time is built with the *current* fixed offset:
  - `assume_offset(ctx.cfg.utc_offset)` in `crates/nugget-proactive/src/generators.rs:848` (renewals)
  - `end_of_day`, `generators.rs:106` (promise and to-do deadlines; "Friday 17:00" in the week of a change is also off by 1 h)
- **Fix direction:** resolve local wall times in the user's IANA zone, or at least with the offset valid on that date. Keep `utc_offset` only as the fallback when no zone is known.

### A3-2. Any refresh invalidates an outstanding preview of an "in two hours" reminder; Approve then fails with an integrity error
- **Severity:** Medium (it surfaces as a broken Approve button)
- **Repro:** `--only binding`.
  1. Preview action 1 (the reminder) of the awaiting-reply card "No answer from Iris Chen".
  2. Call `proactive.refresh`.
  3. Call `cards.execute` with the digest from step 1.
- **Expected:** it executes. Nothing the user saw has changed in substance.
- **Actual:** `{"code": -32005, "message": "card fc6406a22e182988 does not offer this action"}`.
  - Preview label: `Remind me 2026-10-29T15:00:02.239593844Z`
  - After the refresh: `Remind me 2026-10-29T15:00:03.805664327Z`
- **Frequency:** 2/2 runs.
- **When it hits users:** refreshes run every `refresh_minutes` (30), on every ingest change and at startup. Any card whose reminder is `ctx.now + 2h` is exposed:
  - undated promises: `generators.rs:990`
  - awaiting-reply follow-ups: `generators.rs:1097`
  - near-due commitments and to-dos: `generators.rs:450` and `generators.rs:1169`
- **Mechanism:** `refresh_from` replaces `actions` (`crates/nugget-proactive/src/cards.rs:183`). `run_action` then compares the action exactly (`crates/nugget-proactive/src/lib.rs:501`, `matches_offered`).
- **Fix direction:** make relative times stable per card, e.g. computed from `card.created_at` or rounded to the hour, or keep existing actions when only `now`-derived fields change. The client should also map this error to "preview again" rather than a failure.

### A3-3. Local reminders and focus blocks ask for confirmation with false claims ("send something on your behalf", "spend money")
- **Severity:** Medium (it trains users to click through confirmations, and the dry-run says risk `edit`)
- **Repro:** `--only exec`. Execute with `confirmed=false` on:
  - the to-do reminder ("Submit the October expense report…")
  - its focus event
  - Maya's promise reminder ("Sure, I'll send the vendor shortlist…")
  - the renewal reminder
- **Expected:** a local reminder or a `.ics` without attendees is risk `edit`, which is what the preview says. It should execute, or at most ask with an accurate reason.
- **Actual:** `needs_confirmation`, with these reasons:
  - "Nugget is about to send something on your behalf in reminders: create_reminder “Submit the October expense report …”. Treated as send (label matches send keyword “submit”)."
  - "Nugget is about to spend money or commit you to something in reminders: create_reminder “Renewal: Inkwell Studio — $96.00” … (label matches spend keyword “renew”)."
  - The policy log records `"risk":"send"` with `rule default:confirm-risky` (`exec-v0-audit.json`).
  - The preview still showed `risk: "edit"`, and nothing in the dry run says a confirmation is coming.
- **Frequency:** 4/5 local reminders/events in this corpus, in both runs.
- **Owning code:**
  - the label-keyword raise, `crates/nugget-policy/src/classify.rs:211`
  - which is fed the reminder text as `target_label`/`text_preview` by `Action::descriptor`, `crates/nugget-proactive/src/actions.rs` (around line 318)
- **Fix direction:**
  - Don't apply UI-label keyword escalation to local-only proactive targets (`reminders`, `vault`, and `calendar` without attendees), or classify the verb, not the user's text.
  - Return the policy's effective risk or decision in `cards.preview`, so the dry run says what will happen.

### A3-4. archive_thread with no source that can archive: Nugget asks the user to confirm a destructive-sounding action that cannot run, then fails
- **Severity:** Medium/Low
- **Repro:** `--only exec`. The files source's unanswered card offers `archive_thread` with `supported: false`. Preview it, then execute with `confirmed=false`, then with `confirmed=true`.
- **Expected:** preview/execute refuse up front as unsupported. The user is never asked to confirm.
- **Actual:**
  - The preview succeeds.
  - Unconfirmed execute returns `needs_confirmation`: "Nugget is about to delete or overwrite data in mail: archive_thread “Packaging dieline questions”." This contradicts the diff's "Messages are kept; they only leave the inbox."
  - Only after the user confirms: `-32002 "none of these messages is in a mailbox that can archive"`. The card stays open (good).
- **Frequency:** 2/2 runs.
- **Owning code:**
  - `Core::preview` / `Core::execute` in `crates/nuggetd/src/features/proactive.rs:565-616` never consult `exec.supports()`
  - the runner's executor check (`crates/nugget-proactive/src/actions.rs:642`) only checks that *a* mail executor exists, and the daemon always has one
- **Fix direction:** return `Unsupported` from preview/execute when `!exec.supports(action)`. Reword the destroy reason for archive.

### A3-5. Proactive drafts present the user's own words as the other person's message
- **Severity:** Medium (draft quality on follow_up cards: 2 of the 3 draft_reply cards here)
- **Repro:** `--only exec`, then look at `qa/GG4/out/a3/fake_llm.jsonl`.
- **What the drafter sends for Owen's card:** `<thread><message from="other">Sure, I'll send the vendor shortlist by Friday.</message></thread>` with `<instruction>Follow through on: "Sure, I'll send…"</instruction>`. Maya's question to Iris is also sent as `from="other"`.
- **Expected:** these are Maya's own messages (`from="me"`).
- **Why it matters:** a real model may thank Owen for *his* promise, or answer Maya's own question.
- **Frequency:** 2/2 follow_up drafts per run, both runs.
- **Owning code:**
  - `field_request`, `crates/nuggetd/src/features/proactive/exec/draft.rs:108-113` (`from_me: Some(false)` for every evidence quote)
  - `DraftRequest.context` is `Vec<String>` and loses who wrote each quote
- **Fix direction:** carry author/`from_user` per evidence item into `DraftRequest`. For follow_up drafts, also include the other side's last message.

### A3-6. With no model, draft_reply is still marked `supported: true`
- **Severity:** Low
- **Repro:** `--only nomodel` (no provider, runtime off).
- **Actual:** the card lists `{"kind":"draft_reply","supported":true}`. The preview then fails in 0.0 s with `-32001 "no provider is available for the Synthesis tier"`.
- **Assessment:** the failure is fast and names the problem, but "Synthesis tier" is jargon a user cannot act on. Approving the card's other action still works.
- **Frequency:** 2/2 runs.
- **Owning code:** `Exec::supports`, `crates/nuggetd/src/features/proactive/exec.rs:254` (`self.drafter.is_some()`, always true).
- **Fix direction:** report supported=false, or "needs a model", when no chat provider can serve the tier. Use a user-facing message ("Install or connect a model to draft replies").

### A3-7. Reminder labels and diffs are raw RFC 3339, sometimes UTC with nanoseconds
- **Severity:** Low
- **Repro:** `--only exec`.
- **Examples:**
  - `Remind me 2026-10-29T15:00:02.239593844Z` for a user in New York, where it means 11:00 local
  - `Reminder at 2026-10-29T15:00:05.274036156Z: Follow up with …`
  - Event diffs show `When: 2026-10-29T18:00:00-04:00 – …`
- **Frequency:** 1 of 4 reminders per run carries UTC and nanoseconds; all labels are machine format.
- **Owning code:**
  - `fmt_time`, `crates/nugget-proactive/src/actions.rs:169`
  - `ctx.now + Duration::hours(2)` is never rounded (`generators.rs:990, 1097, 1169, 450`)
- **Fix direction:** round relative times to the minute or quarter hour, express them in the user's offset, and let the desktop format them.

### A3-8. `reminders.list` and `reminder.due` never carry the card id
- **Severity:** Low
- **Repro:** `--only exec`.
- **Actual:** every stored reminder has `"card_id": null` (4/4), including the `reminder.due` payloads, although `ReminderView.card_id` is documented as "The card it came from". The app cannot link a notification back to its card.
- **Owning code:** `crates/nuggetd/src/features/proactive/exec.rs:563` (`self.reminders.add(reminder, None)`). `ReminderExecutor::create_reminder` gets no card id.
- **Fix direction:** pass the card id through the `Reminder` action or the executor call.

### A3-9. The kill switch does not stop previews: drafting calls the model while halted
- **Severity:** Low
- **Repro:** `--only binding`. Call `policy.kill`, then `cards.preview` on a draft_reply.
- **Actual:** the preview succeeds and the fake LLM receives a request: 6 requests in `fake_llm.jsonl`, one of them during the halt. Execute is correctly `halted`.
- **Owning code:** the halt is only checked in `ActionRunner::execute_claimed`, `crates/nugget-proactive/src/actions.rs:634`.
- **Fix direction:** decide whether "stops every agent now" covers model calls made for a preview. If it does, check `policy.halted()` in `Core::preview` / `ActionRunner::prepare`.

### N1. research_note could not be tested end to end (not offered)
- **Severity:** Info; this is the lead's known meeting_prep issue.
- **What happens:** the external meeting `.ics` (ORGANIZER/ATTENDEE `bea@harbourline.example`, tomorrow 14:00) is ingested, but `gg4-probe` shows `"attendees": []`, so no meeting_prep card and no ResearchNote action.
- **From reading the code:**
  - `Vault::write_note` (`crates/nugget-vault/src/lib.rs:399-417`) never overwrites. A second note with the same title becomes `Notes/<title> 2.md`.
  - The dry run says only `New note: Prep: …` and does not show the path that will actually be written.

### N2. Commitment "Focus" event lands at 18:00 the evening before the due date
- **Severity:** Low, design. The ics/diff times themselves match exactly.
- **What happens:** for a to-do due 2026-10-30, the focus block is `2026-10-29T18:00–19:00 -04:00` (NY) or `2026-11-04T18:00Z` (London), which is after working hours. The cause is `remind_at = due(17:00) - 1 day`, then `+1 h` and `replace_minute(0)` (`generators.rs:1169-1173`).
- **Also from reading the code (not observed):** when `remind_at = now + 2h`, `replace_minute(0)` keeps the seconds and nanoseconds, so the event would start at e.g. `11:00:05.27`.

## What works well
- **Approval binding is solid:** every attempt to break it was refused with a clear message, and the refusals changed nothing (`reminders.list` empty, card still `new`). Attempts:
  - random digest
  - another card's digest
  - another action's digest
  - index 7, index -1
  - reuse after execute
  - preview or execute on a done or dismissed card
  - a digest from before a restart
  - a digest 61 min old
- **TTL boundary is right:** a 58-minute-old digest still executes.
- **Concurrency is safe:** two simultaneous executes of one approval give exactly one reminder (the second gets "unknown or expired preview"). Two different actions of one card at once give exactly one executed and the other "an action for card … is already running".
- **Kill switch:** `halted` with no artifact and the card left open. After `policy.resume` the *same* digest executes.
- **Dry runs match what happens, exactly:**
  - Reminders: stored `at` and text equal the diff to the instant.
  - `.ics`: SUMMARY/DTSTART/DTEND/DESCRIPTION equal the diff, UTC with CRLF, no ATTENDEE/ORGANIZER for a solo event, written under `data/proactive/events/<title>-<id>.ics` (`create_new`, never overwritten).
  - Drafts: artifact text and the `mailto:` to/cc/subject/body equal the diff (cc preserved: `raj@cobaltworks.example`), and nothing is sent.
- **`needs_confirmation` is side-effect free:** retrying the same digest with `confirmed=true` works.
- **Executing marks the card `done` and teaches learning:** sender, thread and domain entries appear, e.g. `lena@cobaltworks.example` +1.5.
- **`reminder.due`:** fires once, never early, within one 60 s tick after `at`, and the reminder leaves `reminders.list`.
- **Time zones in London are correct:** renewal 09:00 GMT the day before, promise at 17:00 on Friday, focus .ics in UTC matching the diff.
