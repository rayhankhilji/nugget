## 3. Findings

### F1. A mail whose From display name contains an unquoted comma ("Paula Grant, RN") never becomes a card. High.
- **Repro:** run nouser, mornings Oct 30 to Nov 3. The input is `qa/GG4/persona/world/files/Mail/Inbox/20261029-1015-s6-nurse.eml` with `From: Paula Grant, RN <paula.grant@riverside-clinic.example>` and the body "Could you call us at 555-0142 to book a follow-up this week?"
- **Expected:** a card "Reply to Paula Grant, RN: Your results - please call" on Oct 30 or 31 (the clinic asks the user to call about starting medication), marked sensitive.
- **Actual:** no card on any morning (S6 "results" 0/5). The `gg4-probe` corpus lists the mail (`"subject": "Your results - please call", "auto": null`) but no candidate.
- **Root cause:** `crates/nugget-extract/src/mail.rs:85` stores only `from.first()`. mail_parser splits the unquoted comma into two mailboxes, so the first one is "Paula Grant" with no address. `Address::parse` then returns None and `crates/nugget-proactive/src/generators.rs:136` (`let Some(from) = &last.from else { continue }`) silently drops the thread. Awaiting, related-threads and the other generators drop it the same way.
- **Proof:** the same mail with `"Paula Grant, RN"` quoted produces the card (nurse run, `out/a1/nurse-day-06.json`): `"title": "Reply to Paula Grant, RN: Your results - please call", "sensitive": false`.
- **Frequency:** 1/1 such mail. Unquoted commas in From ("Last, First", "Name, MD") are common in practice.
- **Fix direction:** in the extractor, keep the first mailbox that has an address, or rejoin the display-name fragments. In the corpus, fall back to `extracted.from` raw text and `Reply-To`. Do not silently drop a thread with no parsable sender; log it.

### F2. HR "Confidential: your 2027 compensation" letter becomes a "Reply to" card and goes into the brief and its notification. High.
- **Repro:** run nouser, mornings Nov 1 to 8 (8/8). The input is `s6-hr.eml`, from `hr@larkspur.example`, body "Maya, your salary for 2027 will be $118,500 (+6%). Please don't share this letter."
- **Expected:** no card (no action asked, truth S6 forbid). If one is shown anyway, it should be sensitive.
- **Actual:** the card `"title": "Reply to Larkspur People Team: Confidential: your 2027 compensation", "sensitive": false, "evidence": ["Maya, your salary for 2027 will be $118,500 (+6%)."]` with a "Draft a reply to hr@larkspur.example" action. It is in `brief.ready` top 5 on Nov 1 and Nov 5 to 8 (`out/a1/nouser-day-07.json` `brief_ready_top`). In the user run, accepting other reply cards lifted it to rank 0.90 on Nov 1, second in the inbox.
- **Root cause:**
  - `text.rs:195` treats any "please ..." as a request. `PLEASE_NOT_ASKING` (`text.rs:172`) has "please don't reply" but not "please don't share" or "please do not forward". Any "please don't/do not" is a prohibition, not a request.
  - `corpus.rs automated_local` has no `hr`, `people` or `payroll` role mailboxes.
  - Sensitivity: see F3.
- **Fix direction:** treat "please don't / please do not / please keep" as not asking. Add HR and payroll role locals. Treat "Confidential" subjects or salary amounts as sensitive (F3).

### F3. Sensitive flag is always false with the runtime off; health, therapy, HR and fraud mail are treated as ordinary. High.
- **Repro:** any run with `[runtime] enabled=false`. Every card on every morning of every run has `"sensitive": false`. That includes "Reply to Rebecca Moss: Thursday session" (mossplace-therapy, Nov 4 to 8), the HR letter, and the nurse card in the nurse run, whose evidence quote is "Hale reviewed your HbA1c result and would like to discuss starting metformin."
- **Root cause:** `crates/nugget-ingest/src/pipeline.rs:519` computes `let sensitive = !ex.pii.is_empty() || triage.is_some_and(|t| t.sensitive)`. Without a local model there is no triage, and the PII regex (card, ssn, iban, secret) misses health, therapy and salary text. `corpus.rs sensitive()` fails closed only when the key is missing, but ingest always writes `false`.
- **Impact:** `judge.rs:70` `judge_allowed(Cloud, allow_cloud=true, sensitive=false)` would send the metformin quote and salary to a cloud judge if the user enables `judge_allow_cloud`. The brief and notification show these titles unflagged.
- **Fix direction:** add a deterministic sensitivity heuristic in ingest that does not need a model: health and clinic terms, therapy, salary or compensation, "confidential", bank, fraud, OTP. Or leave `sensitive` unset (fail closed) when triage did not run, rather than writing `false`.

### F4. A follow-up nudge in the same thread makes the reply card disappear for a day, then come back as a new card. Medium.
- **Repro:** extra run. X6: Priya asks Tue Oct 27 14:00 "Could you review the onboarding copy draft?", then nudges Thu Oct 29 09:30 "Any update on the onboarding copy?" (In-Reply-To).
- **Expected:** one card, open Oct 29 onward, more urgent after the nudge.
- **Actual:** Oct 29 has `d04812050c1a357a` "Reply to Priya Raman: Onboarding copy". On Oct 30 it goes `new->expired` and no card is shown, because the nudge is under 1 day old. Oct 31 has a new id `9cb212d800aa05b6` "Reply to Priya Raman: Re: Onboarding copy". Evidence: `out/a1/extra-day-05.json`, `extra-day-06.json`.
- **Frequency:** 1/1.
- **Root cause:** `generators.rs:159-163` puts the last message id in the dedup key, and the min-age gate at `generators.rs:138` applies to the last message.
- **Impact:** snooze, accept and feedback on the first card are lost, and the user is not told on the morning the person chased.
- **Fix direction:** key the card on the thread. Measure age from the oldest unanswered inbound message. Let a newer inbound raise urgency instead of resetting.

### F5. The "1 day" and "3 days" thresholds are in hours, so asks from yesterday are missing from the 08:00 brief. Medium.
- **Repro:**
  - nouser day 2: Dana's Mon 09:14 ask is not shown Tue 07:59. It is shown at 10:00 (tue10 run: "Reply to Dana Whitfield: Revised checkout mockups"). Maya replied Tue 16:02, so the morning brief never showed it.
  - Day 6: Gus Fri 18:30 "Please confirm" is absent Sat. It appears Sun.
  - Day 4: Jonah awaiting reply (sent Mon 13:30) is absent Thu and appears Fri.
  - Day 9: therapy Mon 08:20 is absent Tue and first appears Wed for a Thursday session.
- **Frequency:** 3 required plus 1 extra miss out of 29 non-meeting expectations, which is every miss outside F1 and meeting prep.
- **Owning code:** `generators.rs:138` (`age < unanswered_min_days`), `generators.rs:1046` (awaiting).
- **Fix direction:** compare local calendar dates (`days_left`-style, using `cfg.utc_offset`), or use "older than the previous brief". The 1-day grace is meant to skip mail the user may still answer the same day. A mail from yesterday has had that chance.

### F6. A cold recruiter email ranks at or above family and client items and climbs for two weeks; learning from accepts lifts it further. Medium.
- **Repro:** nouser days 4 to 14. "Reply to Kelsey Vance: Senior designer role at a fintech?" goes from .76 to .85. It is above "Reply to Ngozi Okafor: Thanksgiving" (.72 to .77) and above the HR and therapy items, and is second on Nov 7 and 8.
- **User run:** after the user accepted the Harriet, Thanksgiving and Gus reply cards, Kelsey reached **.92: second on Oct 31 and the top open card on Nov 1**. It stays .84 to .88 to the end. Its later meeting invite was cancelled on Oct 30.
- **Owning code:** urgency rises linearly with age (`generators.rs:209`). Importance for an unknown sender is 0 but does not penalise. The kind-level accept boost in `feedback.rs` applies across senders.
- **Truth note:** "acceptable only ranked low".
- **Fix direction:** add a first-contact or cold-outreach penalty for senders the user never wrote to who are not in the graph. Cap urgency growth for low-importance senders. Do not let a kind-level accept lift a never-seen sender.

### F7. A question to someone else with the user on Cc becomes a "Reply to" card. Medium/Low.
- **Repro:** extra run. X4: Priya to Theo, Cc Maya, "Theo, can you approve the illustration contractor invoice by Friday?"
- **Actual:** "Reply to Priya Raman: Illustration invoice" (.72 to .79), open Oct 30 to Nov 5 (7/7 mornings).
- **Owning code:** `generators.rs:141-144` accepts `cc` as equivalent to direct. It only lowers the `direct` feature, which the score barely uses.
- **Fix direction:** when the user is only on Cc, require the ask to name or address the user. Otherwise drop it or rank it below min_score.

### F8. "No answer from Sofia: Re: drinks friday?" appears three days after the drinks happened and stays for a week. Low.
- **Repro:** nouser days 8 to 14. Maya's reply "Yes! 7pm?" ends in "?", so the awaiting-reply generator treats it as an open question. Forbidden per truth S7, 7 card-mornings.
- **Owning code:** `generators.rs` `awaiting_replies` (around line 1030).
- **Fix direction:** skip awaiting cards when the question is about a dated plan whose date has passed ("friday" in the subject is in the past). Ignore short confirmations to personal-domain friends.

### F9. The renewal card is still offered, ranked top, the day after the charge date, with its title in the future tense. Low.
- **Repro:** nouser day 10 (Wed Nov 4, London). The top card is "Pixelboard renews 2026-11-03 — $144.00" (.97). It expires Nov 5.
- **Owning code:** `generators.rs:852` (`at < now - 1 day`) and `generators.rs:893` (`expires_at = at + 1 day`).
- **Fix direction:** expire at the charge time. If it is shown after the date, word it "renewed on Nov 3" with low rank.

### F10. The bank fraud alert ("Reply YES if this was you") produces no card or brief item. Low (judgement call).
- **Repro:** `s6-bank.eml` from `alerts@firstharbor-bank.example` is dropped as an automated sender on every morning.
- **Assessment:** this is correct noise suppression in general. But an urgent account-security question is the kind of item a personal assistant should surface, marked sensitive.
- **Owning code:** `corpus.rs automated_local` ("alerts").
- **Fix direction:** add an allow-path for fraud or security alerts from a domain the user has transacted with.

### F11. Readability and lifecycle polish. Low.
- Answered items and timed-out items both end as `expired` in `cards_all` (`lib.rs:303` and `lib.rs:310`). Metrics and UI cannot tell "you handled it" apart from "it lapsed". Suggest a `resolved` state or reason.
- "wrote N days ago" floors elapsed hours. Rebecca wrote Mon 08:20 and on Thu 07:59 London the card says "wrote 2 days ago". Ngozi wrote Wed evening and on Sat it says "2 days".
- The evidence quote `lead()` splits at "Dr.": "Hale reviewed your HbA1c result..." (`text.rs:126` `sentences`). It also picks the diagnosis sentence rather than the ask ("Could you call us...").
- Commitment title repeats the date: "Due 2026-11-04: Send Jonah the final brochure files by Nov 4". Titles use ISO dates ("renews 2026-11-03"). Renewal reminder labels use a local offset (`2026-11-02T09:00:00-04:00`), while follow-up reminders use raw UTC nanoseconds (known).
- After the London switch, the X2 promise's `due` changed from `2026-11-03T17:00:00-05:00` to `2026-11-03T17:00:00Z`, because the floating "end of day" is re-anchored to the current offset each refresh (`generators.rs` `end_of_day`). It is harmless here (already past) but could shift an upcoming deadline by the zone delta.

Harness caveat: note dates (`todo.md`) come from file mtime, which is real time 2026-10-30. For mornings before that, notes would be skipped as "future" (`generators.rs` `note_todos`: `n.date > ctx.now`). S10 was only checked from Nov 2, so it is unaffected. `replay.py` should `os.utime` copied files to their `at`.

## 4. What works well
- **Resolution on reply is reliable:** 6/6 handled situations left the inbox the next morning (Dana delivered, Jonah replied, Harriet, Gus, Theo, Thanksgiving), and accepted cards moved to `done`.
- **Follow-ups on the user's own promises are good:**
  - "You told Dana Whitfield: 'Yes, I'll send the revised mockups by Thursday.'" appears 2 days ahead and disappears after delivery.
  - The budget promise stays through the overdue weekend and is in `brief.overdue`.
  - Late-evening promises resolve deadlines in the writer's own zone. X1 "by Friday" sent Thu 21:40 EDT gives due `2026-10-30T17:00-04:00`, not Nov 6. X2 "tomorrow" sent Mon 20:30 EST gives due Nov 3.
- **Renewals:** "Pixelboard renews 2026-11-03 — $144.00" from Oct 28 using the Oct 3 receipt as proof. The Cloudvault trial (never paid, List-Unsubscribe) is never shown.
- **Noise:** 0 cards from newsletters, promos, CI (Auto-Submitted), the OOO auto-reply, `Accepted:`, the airline check-in, the lab portal notice and the OTP across 14 days. The FYI forward (X3) correctly makes no card.
- **Dedup:** the S9 same Message-ID in Inbox and Clients gives one card. A late Archive copy (X5) does not resurrect the answered thread. 0 duplicate cards in all runs.
- **S10 note todo** "Due 2026-11-04: Send Jonah the final brochure files by Nov 4" appears Nov 2 and becomes "Overdue since 2026-11-04" on Nov 5.
- **Feedback:** dismissing with `already_handled` or `not_relevant` removes the card and it does not come back (HR and Sofia drinks in the user run).
- **Reliability:** `brief.ready` fired 14/14 mornings in every run, including the DST Sunday and the London days.
