# GG4 / A4: the morning brief, its timing and time zones

Script: `qa/GG4/scenarios/brief.py` (`--out DIR [--only PARTS] [--runtime --chat-model --embed-model --models-home] [--judge] [--reuse]`).
Parts: `accuracy` (14 persona mornings, TZ follows the trip), `fixed` (OS zone left on New York for the London days), `explicit` (`[proactive] utc_offset = "+00:00"` for the trip), `timing`, `runover` (x600 over two nights), `dst` (x600 Sat 20:00 EDT to Mon 08:00 EST, snooze, 01:30 brief), `zoneswap` (x600, OS zone file swapped NY to London mid-flight), `config`, `edge` (synthetic edge events), `snooze`.
The ground truth for "today's meetings" is computed in the script from the .ics files that had arrived by each morning. It does not use the product's parser. It handles TZID, VALUE=DATE, weekly/daily RRULE, UID+SEQUENCE, METHOD:CANCEL/STATUS:CANCELLED and the user's PARTSTAT=DECLINED. A timed event that ended before the brief fired is optional.

Runs: deterministic, `[runtime]` off, judge off, binary `target/debug/nuggetd` (built 14:40 on top of 5f1998e). Speed-ups use `FAKETIME="@<epoch> xN"` with the monotonic clock faked too (class `SpeedDaemon`). I checked that libfaketime 0.9.10 scales `epoll_wait`/`nanosleep` and that child `date +%z` processes keep the sped-up clock. Futex timed waits are not scaled, but tokio's timers do not depend on them.
Evidence: `qa/GG4/out/a4/` (summary.json: 53 checks, 33 pass; per-day JSON for the three accuracy modes; edge result; daemon log excerpts for DST and the zone swap). Full output, including homes and logs, is in the scratchpad `a4/run-*` directories.

## Findings

### F1. Moved and cancelled meetings stay in the brief: duplicates and ghosts (High)
- **Repro:** `python3 qa/GG4/scenarios/brief.py --out /tmp/a4 --only accuracy`, or `--only edge` for a quicker look at the field loss. Then inspect `accuracy-os/days.json` for 2026-10-30 and 2026-11-06.
- **Expected:** Oct 30: one "Northwind design QA" at 15:00. It was moved from 13:00 (same UID, SEQUENCE 1). Nov 6: no "Coffee with Kelsey" (METHOD:CANCEL, STATUS:CANCELLED, SEQUENCE 1).
- **Actual:** the brief.ready payload contains:
  - Oct 30: `Northwind design QA 2026-10-30T13:00:00-04:00` **and** `Northwind design QA 2026-10-30T15:00:00-04:00`.
  - Nov 6: `Coffee with Kelsey (TalentBridge) 2026-11-06T09:00:00Z` ×2. One copy is the original invite; the other is the cancellation notice itself.

  `gg4-probe` shows `cancelled: false` for `ev-kelsey-cancel.ics`. Every meeting also has `location: null` (e.g. "Northwind office, 5th floor", "Zoom"). Frequency: 2/2 mornings that have a moved or cancelled event (2/14 mornings). The same thing happens in the fixed and explicit trip runs.
- **Code:** there are two causes.
  1. `crates/nugget-extract/src/ics.rs:329-336` promotes only `uid, start, end, timezone, all_day` from `extracted.events[0]` to `extracted`. `crates/nugget-proactive/src/corpus.rs:683-707` reads `extracted.status`, `extracted.location` and `extracted.organizer` (and attendees, which the lead already reported), so those fields are always missing.
  2. Nothing reconciles events by UID. `corpus.rs:255` pushes every item and `brief.rs:102-104` lists them all.
- **Fix:** read the fields from `extracted.events[]`, or promote them. Group events by UID, keep the one with the highest SEQUENCE (then the latest DTSTAMP), and drop the UID when that winner is METHOD:CANCEL or STATUS:CANCELLED. Handle RECURRENCE-ID overrides the same way once RRULE is expanded.

### F2. Times computed before a DST change use the old offset: snooze, reminders, due dates (Medium)
- **Repro:** `python3 qa/GG4/scenarios/brief.py --out /tmp/a4 --only dst` (about 6 min).
- **Expected:** Sat Oct 31 20:00 EDT, snooze with no `until` → "tomorrow at brief time" = 2026-11-01 08:00 EST (13:00Z). The Pixelboard renewal (Nov 3) reminder is at 09:00 local on Nov 2.
- **Actual:**
  - `snoozed_until: "2026-11-01T08:00:00-04:00"`, which is 07:00 EST. The card is back to `new` an hour before the brief.
  - Card actions computed Oct 31: `"Remind me 2026-11-02T09:00:00-04:00"` (08:00 EST) and `due 2026-11-03T09:00:00-04:00`.

  Frequency: deterministic, 1/1 run. It affects every future time created before a DST change.
- **Related:** the deadline instant of a card moves whenever the offset changes, because it is re-derived at each refresh. The budget promise "by Friday" has due `2026-10-30T17:00:00-04:00` on Oct 31 and `2026-10-30T17:00:00-05:00` on Nov 1. The note to-do is due `2026-11-04T17:00:00Z` while she is in London and `-05:00` once she is home.
- **Code:** `crates/nuggetd/src/features/proactive/settings.rs:135-138` (`default_snooze_until` applies today's offset to tomorrow). `crates/nugget-proactive/src/generators.rs:103-107, 846-849, 880` (`assume_offset(cfg.utc_offset)` on future dates; `at - 1 day`).
- **Fix:** keep an IANA zone, from `TZ` / `/etc/localtime` or a `[proactive] timezone`, and resolve each local wall time with that zone's rules for its date (`time-tz`/`jiff`). A single `UtcOffset` cannot do this. The brief clock is already right because it re-asks `date +%z` before firing. The other times need the same treatment.

### F3. Brief meetings cannot be traced to their source (Medium)
- **Repro:** any brief, e.g. `--only accuracy`; look at `meetings[]`.
- **Expected:** each item in the brief can be traced to its evidence. Top, overdue and renewals do this through `card_id` → evidence (85/85 resolve, quotes present in the files).
- **Actual:** `BriefMeetingView` has only `title, start, end, all_day, location, attendees, external, prep_card, prep`. There is no item ref, uid or url, so 12/12 listed meetings cannot be opened or verified. `prep` is always empty because there are no prep cards (attendees, known).
- **Code:** `crates/nugget-core/src/ipc.rs:1660-1675`, `crates/nugget-proactive/src/brief.rs:53-68,108-130`.
- **Fix:** add `item: ItemRef` (and `url`, `uid`) to `BriefMeeting`/`BriefMeetingView`.

### F4. Invalid `[proactive]` config silently turns off all proactive features (Medium)
- **Repro:** `python3 qa/GG4/scenarios/brief.py --out /tmp/a4 --only config` (3 s). Cases: `brief_time` 25:00 / 8am / 08:00:00 / "" / 8, `utc_offset` EST / America/New_York / +5, and an unknown key `timezone`.
- **Expected:** a typo in brief_time does not quietly stop cards and the brief. Either the daemon refuses to start, or `status`/`brief.get` say why.
- **Actual:** the daemon runs. `brief.get` and `cards.list` → `-32601: method not found: brief.get`. `status` → `{"audit_chain_ok":true,"chunks":0,...}` with no feature error. The reason is only in `data/logs/nuggetd.*.log`: `{"message":"feature did not start","feature":"proactive","error":"invalid input: [proactive]: brief_time \"25:00\" is not HH:MM"}`. Frequency: 9/9 invalid cases. The messages themselves are good: they name the section, the value and the expected format.
- **Also:** `utc_offset = "America/New_York"` (a natural thing to write) is rejected, and there is no `timezone` key. A traveller therefore can only pin a fixed offset, which then ignores DST.
- **Code:** `crates/nuggetd/src/features/mod.rs:139`, `crates/nuggetd/src/features/proactive/settings.rs:95-104`.
- **Fix:** surface failed features in `status` (name + error). Answer proactive methods with a typed "feature unavailable: <reason>" error instead of method-not-found. Consider accepting IANA names.

### F5. TZID ignored: impact on the brief (known; quantified) (Medium, already reported)
- With TZ following the trip, "Studio retro" (16:00 America/New_York) shows on Nov 6 at 16:00 London instead of 21:00.
- With the OS zone left on New York, "Foundry workshop" (10:00 Europe/London) shows at 15:00 London.
- The red-eye ends `2026-11-04T07:00:00-05:00` (12:00 London) instead of 07:00 London, so the flight is 12 h long instead of 7 h.
- Edge: a 10:00 America/Los_Angeles call shows at 10:00 EST.

Wrong-time meetings by mode: 1/14 mornings (os), 1/4 (fixed), 1/4 (explicit). Floating times (Run club) and `Z` times are handled correctly. The ics extractor already stores `timezone` and `end_timezone` (`ics.rs:383-397`), but `corpus.rs:620-632` never reads them.

### F6. Past renewals stay in "Renewals in the next 7 days", and renewals are listed twice (Low)
- **Repro:** `--only accuracy`, Nov 4.
- **Actual:** `renewals: ["Pixelboard renews 2026-11-03 — $144.00"]` on 2026-11-04, the day after it renewed. On every day it shows (8/8), the same card is also in `top`, so it appears twice in one brief.
- **Code:** `crates/nugget-proactive/src/brief.rs:145-149` (only `due <= now + 7d`, no lower bound) and `brief.rs:151-158` (Top excludes meeting prep and overdue but not renewals).
- **Fix:** use `now <= due <= now+7d` (or a short grace period), and exclude cards already placed in another section from Top.

### F7. On a fresh install, the first day's brief is spent on an empty corpus (Low)
- **Repro:** `--only timing`, step 9. Empty home at 07:59:45: no brief.ready (by design), but the day is marked as fired. The source is added at 09:00 and synced.
- **Actual:** no brief.ready that day, although `brief.get` then has 2 top cards. The zone-swap run shows the same thing: a fresh install at 18:00 fires an empty catch-up brief (log `morning brief ready date=2026-11-03 cards=0`) before the first sync finishes.
- **Code:** `crates/nuggetd/src/features/proactive.rs:741-756`. `set_brief_mark(today)` runs even when `worth_announcing` is false.
- **Fix:** do not set the mark for an unannounced empty brief, or wait for the first ingest to finish before the first brief.

### F8. A missed brief fires at any hour (Low)
- **Repro:** `--only timing`, step 7. The machine was off at 08:00 and the daemon started at 22:30.
- **Actual:** brief.ready at `2026-10-30T22:30:15-04:00`.
- **Code:** `settings.rs:143-162` (`next_brief_at` returns `now` once the brief time has passed).
- **Fix:** catch up only within a window, e.g. until noon local or brief_time + N h. After that, skip to tomorrow, or show the brief silently without notifying.

### F9. Multi-day all-day events appear only on their first day; reminder labels show raw UTC (Low)
- **Multi-day all-day:** "Design conference (3 days)", DATE Nov 8 to Nov 11, is absent from the Nov 9 brief. `brief.rs:103` matches on the start date only. Use overlap with the local day for all-day events, and perhaps for in-progress timed events.
- **Reminder labels:** card actions read `Remind me 2026-10-30T14:00:00.001543752Z` (UTC with nanoseconds) and `Remind me 2026-11-02T09:00:00-04:00`. See `crates/nugget-proactive/src/actions.rs:265`. Format the label in local time, e.g. "Remind me Fri 10:00".

### Observations (not failures)
- **OS zone not changed while abroad** (`trip.fixed.*`): the brief fires at 08:00 New York = 13:00 London. That is correct for the configured zone, but late for her. Changing the OS zone works even while the daemon runs: the offset change is picked up within the 15-minute recheck, and the brief then fires at 08:00:00 GMT on Nov 4 with "today" = 11-04.
- **Pinning `utc_offset = "+00:00"`** fixes the trip. If she forgets to remove it after flying home, the Nov 8 brief fires at 03:00 EST, with no hint in the brief or status that the offset is pinned.
- **Overnight event** (23:00 Sun to 02:00 Mon) is not listed on Monday. This is acceptable because it is over by 08:00.

## What works well
- **The brief clock is exact and robust:**
  - 14/14 mornings fire at 08:00:00–08:00:01 local, in New York and in London.
  - Exactly one per day across restarts (08:10 and 00:30 restarts are quiet) and across two simulated nights (x600: 10-27/28/29 at 08:00:00).
  - brief_time changes (09:15, "7:05") are honoured. Moving brief_time later on a day that already fired does not repeat the brief.
- **DST handling for the brief itself is right.** A daemon running from Saturday evening through the fall-back fires Sunday at 08:00:00 EST and Monday at 08:00:00 EST, not 07:00. The forced offset re-check before firing (`proactive.rs:729-736`) works. A brief_time of 01:30 fires once on the night when 01:30 happens twice. The log shows the offset change.
- **Local "today" is right at the UTC boundary.** A `Z` event at 03:00Z on Nov 10 is listed on Nov 9 at 22:00 EST. 03:00Z on Nov 9 (Sunday night local) is not listed. Events just after midnight tomorrow are not listed. Mom's birthday appears only on Oct 31 and the brief date is the local date.
- **Top/overdue/renewal items are fully traceable** (85/85): card → evidence file exists, the `file://` url exists, and the quoted sentence is in the file.
- **The overdue section is accurate:** the budget promise appears exactly Oct 31–Nov 2 and nothing is shown before its due time.
- **Renewals are not over-eager:** the Cloudvault trial never appears.
- **No invented meetings:** 0/12, and meetings are always sorted.
- **`enabled = false`** gives no notification, an empty brief and no cards.
- **Config error messages** name the section, the value and the expected format (they are just not surfaced; see F4).
