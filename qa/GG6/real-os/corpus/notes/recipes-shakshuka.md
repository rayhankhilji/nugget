# Shakshuka (the version Mira likes)

Serves 3. Two tins of tomatoes, one red pepper, one onion, 5 eggs, cumin, smoked paprika, a pinch of chilli.
Cook the pepper and onion 10 minutes, add spices, then tomatoes for 15 minutes, crack the eggs in and cover for 7 minutes.
Mira prefers it with feta and without coriander.
