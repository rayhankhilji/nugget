# GP visit, Halden Bay Medical Practice

Saw Dr. Lena Brandt about the tiredness and the breathlessness on the climbing wall.

Blood results came back: ferritin 9, haemoglobin low. Dr. Brandt says it's iron-deficiency
anaemia, probably diet plus heavy periods, nothing scary.

Prescription: ferrous sulfate 200 mg, one tablet twice a day with orange juice, for three
months. Avoid tea within an hour of taking it. Repeat bloods in 12 weeks.

Told Theo; he's going to stop buying so much tea. #health
