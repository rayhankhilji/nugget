# Sunday 15 March

Theo and I have been together three years this month. He made the cardamom buns his gran used
to make and we ate them in bed reading the papers. My partner is, it turns out, a very good
baker.

Harbor is officially mine now. Bob made it sound like a gift; it is mostly a migration plan
and a lot of meetings. Sam (Patel) seems sharp, which helps.

Climbing with Sam Thursday as usual. My forearms still haven't recovered from last week.

Still love living in Halden Bay: the sea at the end of the road never gets old. #journal
