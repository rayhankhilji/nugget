# Thanksgiving 2026 - plan

Hosting at my place this year, Thursday, November 26. Twelve people confirmed
(Aunt Lorraine still a maybe).

## Menu
- Turkey, 18 lb, dry-brined two days ahead (start Tuesday night)
- Cornbread-sausage stuffing, baked outside the bird
- Mashed potatoes with roasted garlic
- Sweet potato casserole, pecan topping, no marshmallows this time
- Green beans with crispy shallots
- Cranberry-orange relish (make Wednesday, it keeps)
- Dessert: pumpkin pie, plus Danny's crème brûlée if he remembers the torch

## Timeline
- Sat Nov 21: big grocery run, pick up turkey (pre-ordered at Halsey's Market)
- Tue: thaw check, mix dry brine (kosher salt, brown sugar, sage, pepper)
- Wed Nov 25: pies, relish, chop everything, set the table
- Thu: turkey in at 10:30, rest 40 min, carve at 3:15, eat at 3:30
