## Candidate findings (most severe first)

### B-1: The newest email in a thread is cut from the front, so the question is dropped and only the quoted history is kept (High)

- **Repro:** run the scripted draft above, then read `result.prompt.user` for
  `qual-mw-renewal-thread` and `qual-mw-long-thread-22`.
- **Expected:** Priya's new text is in the prompt: "I need two things in writing: the final annual
  fee that finance approved, and the confirmed start date…". In the long thread, Tom's question
  about "the fuel surcharge … and the new estimated delivery date for the CRV-5521 containers" is
  in the prompt.
- **Actual:** each thread message is capped with `untrusted_tail(m.text, message_chars=600)`, which
  keeps the **end** of the message. In an email reply the end is the quoted history. The newest
  message in `qual-mw-renewal-thread` is rendered as:
  ```
  <message from="other" author="Priya Raman …" time="2026-10-29 17:31">
  …our legal team has completed its final read-through.
  › Kind regards,
  › Marcus
  › On 13 Oct 2026, at 16:40, Priya Raman wrote:
  › › Hi Marcus, …
  ```
  The question is not in the prompt at all. In `qual-mw-long-thread-22`, the 1,200-character
  thread budget keeps 2 of 22 messages. The last of them is the quoted tail of Ines's older
  message ("…vided at least three working days before departure…"), and Tom's two questions are
  gone. A real model can only answer by luck, from the facts block.
- **Owner:** `crates/nugget-draft/src/prompt.rs`, `user_text` (thread loop) and `untrusted_tail`.
  Local limits are set in `PromptLimits::local`.
- **Fix direction:**
  - Strip the quoted history from each message before capping: lines starting with `>` or `›`,
    and everything after "On … wrote:", "-----Original Message-----" or "From:".
  - Cap a message from its head, not its tail.
  - Always give the newest message from someone else the largest share of the budget.

### B-2: Date phrases in other people's messages become date filters on retrieval, which drops the fact the reply needs (High)

- **Repro:** run `qual-mw-payment` and `qual-sb-email-screen-time`, then rerun each with the date
  phrase removed. The A/B scenarios are in
  `qa/GG1/results/B-quality-ab/ab.json`:
  "last week" is deleted, and "our call on Tuesday" is changed to "our call next week".
- **Expected:** the payment note ("received 24 October 2026") and the calendar entry ("Tuesday 3
  November 2026, 10:00-10:30 Pacific") are retrieved.
- **Actual:**
  - `qual-mw-payment`: the thread says "We transferred payment … last week". The facts are the
    board dinner, a sent email to Elena and the QBR calendar entry, all written 19–25 Oct. The
    payment note, written 26 Oct, is excluded.
  - `qual-sb-email-screen-time`: the thread asks "what time is our call on Tuesday?". The phrase is
    parsed as the *past* Tuesday (27 Oct), so the only facts are two sent emails dated 27 Oct.
  - In the A/B runs, F1 is "Payment received NW-2026-0917" and F2 is "Calendar: recruiter screen
    with Tomás Ferreira".
- **Owner:** `crates/nugget-draft/src/engine.rs`. `retrieval_query` builds the query from thread
  text, and the engine passes it to `Retriever::pack_context_with`, where
  `nugget-retrieve/src/dates.rs::parse_date_query` reads date phrases as a time range.
- **Fix direction:** add a `ContextOptions` flag that turns off date-phrase parsing, and set it
  for drafts. Thread text is not a search query, and its dates usually describe events rather
  than when the notes were written.

### B-3: Form fields get no facts, and the prompt never says which field it is (High)

- **Repro:** run `qual-jp-form-shipping`: a Chrome checkout page, field "Street address",
  placeholder "Street and number", window "Checkout – Shipping address".
- **Expected:** the shipping address is retrieved and the model knows it is filling in "Street
  address".
- **Actual:**
  - `retrieval_query` uses only the subject, thread and recipients, so the query is empty and
    nothing is retrieved. The prompt has `THREAD_RULES` only.
  - The prompt says only "Draft the value of a single form field … The field is in Google Chrome
    (field kind: form_field)". Field label, placeholder, window title and URL are all missing.
  - The voice block for this field lists Jun's email greetings and sign-offs ("hi mei —",
    "- {my_name}"), which do not belong in a form field.
  - A real model has nothing to fill the field from; it can only guess or write generic text.
- **Owner:** `crates/nugget-draft/src/engine.rs` (`retrieval_query`) and
  `crates/nugget-draft/src/prompt.rs` (`user_text`, `voice_block`).
- **Fix direction:**
  - Add the field label, placeholder and window title to the prompt as untrusted `attr()` values.
  - Use them as the retrieval query when there is no thread or subject.
  - Leave out greetings and sign-offs from the voice block for `form_field`.

### B-4: The user's own sent messages take the few local fact slots and are presented as facts (Medium-High)

- **Repro:** run all scenarios, then count citations with `item.source` in `imap`/`chat`.
- **Expected:** facts come mostly from notes, calendar and files. Old sent emails are already
  used for voice (exemplars).
- **Actual:** 34 of 87 facts come from the user's own old messages, in 25 of 31 prompts. The
  local budget allows only 3 snippets.
  - Two relevant notes lose their slot:
    - In `qual-mw-long-thread-22`, the rate card that holds the 7.5% surcharge is not retrieved.
      F2 and F3 are old sent emails ("Re: Customs paperwork", "Invoice NW-2026-0917").
    - In `qual-sb-email-screen-time`, F1 and F2 are both sent emails.
  - Old sent messages are labelled as facts, which invites the model to repeat old commitments.
    In `qual-mw-placeholder-literal`, F2 is "Dear Tom, … We have reserved four additional
    40-foot containers for the week of 14 September…" on a thread asking for a *new*
    reservation.
  - Likely cause: the greeting and sign-off words in the thread ("Dear Marcus", "Kind regards",
    "Thank you for") match the user's own emails well under text-only retrieval.
- **Owner:** `crates/nugget-draft/src/engine.rs` (`context_future`, `retrieval_query`) and the
  `nugget-retrieve` packing.
- **Fix direction:**
  - Exclude items the user sent from draft retrieval, or rank them below notes.
  - Strip greetings, sign-offs and quoted lines from the query.

### B-5: A newer note that replaces an older one is not marked superseded unless it uses words like "draft" or "final" (Medium)

- **Repro:** run `qual-mw-office-still-havenkade`, then read the facts block.
- **Expected:** F3, the 2025 note "Northwind Freight BV, Havenkade 118 … second floor", is marked
  "superseded by F2".
- **Actual:** both notes are retrieved, but F3 has no superseded mark. F2 says "As of 18 August
  2026 the Rotterdam office is at Wilhelminakade 40 … The Havenkade building is closed."
  `supersedes()` requires an old marker (draft, proposal…) on the older fact and a new marker
  (final, approved…) on the newer one. "Moved", "new address", "no longer", "rescheduled" and
  "changed" are not markers.
- **Draft/final pairs work:** `qual-mw-renewal-thread` (F3 EUR 48,500), `qual-jp-slack-pg-date`
  (F3 7 Nov) and `qual-sb-email-offer-stale` (F2 172,000) are all marked superseded correctly.
- **Owner:** `crates/nugget-draft/src/prompt_facts.rs` (`OLD_MARKERS`, `NEW_MARKERS`,
  `supersedes`).
- **Fix direction:**
  - Treat change words in the newer fact ("moved", "now", "changed", "rescheduled", "replaces",
    "no longer", "closed") as a status change.
  - Or supersede when the titles share subject words, the newer fact is dated later, and the two
    give different values of the same kind (an address or a date).

### B-6: Text the user already typed is repeated in the draft; nothing removes the overlap (Medium)

- **Repro:** run `qual-jp-slack-half-typed` (field value "i'd hold off until ", caret at the end).
- **Expected:** the inserted field reads "i'd hold off until the backfill flake is fixed…".
- **Actual:** `inserted_field` is "i'd hold off until i'd hold off until the backfill flake is
  fixed, it's still failing ~8% on arm64 (TIDE-1377)". Post-processing removes a duplicate
  greeting but not a restated prefix. The prompt's last line also asks for the whole message
  ("Write the chat message now, as the user, in about 20 words") rather than a continuation,
  which encourages a small model to start over.
- **Owner:** `crates/nugget-draft/src/post.rs` (plus `engine.rs::post_options`) and
  `prompt.rs` (closing line).
- **Fix direction:**
  - In `PostOptions`, carry the text before the caret. While deciding the head of the draft, drop
    the longest suffix of that text that the draft starts with, compared case-insensitively and
    ignoring whitespace.
  - When the field is not empty, change the closing line to "Write only the text that continues
    at the caret".

### B-7: Replacing a selection asks for a whole message at the full length band (Medium)

- **Repro:** run `qual-jp-slack-selection` (selected "nov 7, ~2h window", instruction "fix the date
  and window").
- **Expected:** the prompt asks only for the replacement, with a length close to the selection's
  (4 words).
- **Actual:** the prompt includes `<selected_text>` and the instruction, but ends "Write the chat
  message now, as the user, in about 20 words". The length band and `max_words` come from the
  voice median, not from the selection. A small model is likely to write a whole message, and
  the insertion would then read "heads up: prod pg16 upgrade is <whole message>. ping me w/
  questions". The scripted reply was inserted correctly.
- **Owner:** `crates/nugget-draft/src/prompt.rs` (closing line, `LengthBand::for_draft`) and
  `engine.rs` (`post_options`).
- **Fix direction:**
  - With a selection, close with "Write only the replacement for the selected text".
  - Base the length band on the selection: about 2x its words, with a hard cap.
  - Make the output single-line when the selection has no newline.

### B-8: Greeting and sign-off placeholders are not explained or filled in, and the sign-off's line break is lost (Medium)

- **Repro:** read the system prompt of any email scenario, for example `qual-mw-qbr-time`. Then
  run `qual-mw-placeholder-literal`.
- **Expected:** the voice block says "Dear Tom," and "Kind regards,\nMarcus", or explains the
  placeholders. A literal `{my_name}` never reaches the field.
- **Actual:**
  - The voice block shows `Greetings: "Dear {first_name},"` and
    `Sign-offs: "Kind regards, {my_name}"`, and nothing in the prompt says what the placeholders
    mean.
  - `attr()` turns the sign-off's newline into a space, so it is shown as one line, while the user
    always writes it on two lines.
  - The scripted literal output "Dear {first_name}, … Kind regards, {my_name}" is inserted
    unchanged; post-processing has no check for it.
  - Whether a 4B model copies the placeholders must be measured on the real-model run. The
    exemplars, which do show real names, reduce the risk.
- **Owner:** `crates/nugget-draft/src/prompt.rs` (`phrases`, `terse_stats`, `attr`) and
  `post.rs`.
- **Fix direction:**
  - Fill in the first name of a single recipient and the user's own name, which the engine
    already has (recipients, and `MeIdentity` in voice). Otherwise add one line explaining the
    placeholders.
  - Keep the sign-off's line break, for example rendered as `Kind regards,⏎Marcus`.
  - Add a post-processing step that replaces or drops leftover `{…_name}` tokens.

### B-9: Nothing stops a draft from claiming an attachment (Medium; needs a real-model rate)

- **Repro:** run `qual-mw-deck-attachment` and `qual-sb-li-jd-attachment`.
- **Expected:** the draft says the file will follow; it does not say "attached".
- **Actual:** "Please find attached the deck from last quarter's QBR…" and "I've attached the full
  job description here" are inserted unchanged.
  - `SYSTEM_RULES` forbids inventing "facts, dates, numbers, links or commitments" but does not
    mention attachments, although the judge rubric treats an invented attachment as invented.
  - Marcus's own sent mail contains "Please find below" and "Please find our invoice…", so the
    pattern is primed by his voice.
- **Owner:** `crates/nugget-draft/src/prompt.rs` (`SYSTEM_RULES`); optionally `grounding.rs`.
- **Fix direction:**
  - Add a rule: "You can't attach files. Never say something is attached or enclosed; say you
    will send it."
  - Optionally have the grounding check flag "attached" or "enclosed" when the field has no
    attachment.

### B-10: With default settings, values the model invents are not checked (Medium; needs a real-model rate)

- **Repro:** run `qual-mw-missing-insurance`, then read `result.timings.grounding`.
- **Expected:** with grounding on, a value that appears in neither the thread nor the facts is
  flagged or held back.
- **Actual:** "The cargo insurance cover limit for shipment CRV-5521 is EUR 50,000 per container"
  is inserted. The grounding report shows `values_checked: 0` and `values_unsupported: 0`. The
  default `GroundingCheck` only stops copied credentials; checking values is opt-in. This may be
  intentional (latency), but the real-model rate of invented values on
  `qual-mw-missing-insurance`, `qual-jp-slack-missing-url` and `qual-sb-email-visa-missing`
  should decide it.
- **Owner:** `crates/nugget-draft/src/engine.rs` (`DraftConfig::grounding_check` default) and
  `grounding.rs`.
- **Fix direction:** if the real-model run shows invented values, check values (money, dates)
  for email bodies, where a sentence-level hold costs little.

### B-11: Markdown list markers survive in plain-text email bodies (Low)

- **Repro:** run `qual-mw-qbr-time`.
- **Expected:** a plain-text email, or at least no markdown syntax.
- **Actual:** `**Date:**` is stripped, but the list remains: "- Date: Thursday 12 November\n- Time:
  14:00-15:30 CET\n- Room: Maas 2". In `plain_text` mode, `token_clean` handles `**`, `__`,
  `~~`, backticks and `#` headings, but not `- ` / `* ` / `1. ` list markers.
- **Owner:** `crates/nugget-draft/src/post.rs` (`token_clean`).
- **Fix direction:** for chat messages, fold list items into the sentence. For email, plain "- "
  lines are acceptable, so this may be a no-op by design; at least strip `* ` bullets.

### B-12: Cutting a draft at the length cap removes its sign-off (Low)

- **Repro:** run `qual-mw-support-ticket` (scripted reply of about 300 words).
- **Expected:** a cut draft still ends like an email: "Kind regards,\nMarcus".
- **Actual:** the draft is cut at a sentence end after 112 words ("…no additional cost to Corvid
  Labs."), with no sign-off. The cap is the persona's overall email median (43 words), so
  `hard_max` is 129 words. `done.stop_reason` is reported as `EndTurn`.
- **Owner:** `crates/nugget-draft/src/post.rs` (`cut_to_words`) and `engine.rs`.
- **Fix direction:**
  - For email bodies, append the learned sign-off after a cut, unless the field has one below
    the caret.
  - Report a distinct stop reason for a cut.

### B-13: Irrelevant facts are always added (Low)

- **Repro:** run `qual-jp-slack-thanks-nofact` (Ana: "thanks for jumping on the incident last
  night 🙏").
- **Expected:** no facts block, or only relevant facts.
- **Actual:** three unrelated facts are added: the on-call rotation, the Postgres decision, and

- **Owner:** `engine.rs` (`keep_best_snippets`) and `nugget-retrieve`.

### B-14: The chat guidance contradicts the learned LinkedIn voice (Low)

- **Repro:** read the prompt of `qual-sb-li-salary`.
- **Actual:** the chat-message guidance says "no sign-off", while the voice block lists the
  learned sign-offs `"Best, {my_name}", "{my_name}"` and both exemplars end with "Best,\nSofia" or

- **Owner:** `prompt.rs` (`kind_guidance`).
- **Fix direction:** say "a sign-off only if the user's voice shows one" (as the email guidance

### B-15: Length and tone in the voice block are off (Low)

- **Length:** in `qual-mw-renewal-thread`, Priya has 4 sent samples, fewer than the 5 that
  `LengthBand::for_draft` requires, so the target falls back to the kind default ("about 90
  words"). Marcus's overall email median is 43 words (the support-ticket prompt shows "about 43
  words"). Fall back to the channel or overall median instead.
- **Tone:** Marcus writes with no contractions, "Dear …", "I would be grateful" and "Kind
  regards", yet the voice block calls his tone "neutral" (formality < 0.7).
- **Owner:** `prompt.rs` (`LengthBand::for_draft`) and the `nugget-voice` stats formality
  estimate.

## What works well (protect these)

- **Preamble removal:** "Here is a draft reply in Marcus's voice:\n\n" is removed, and the
  inserted text starts "Dear Priya," (`qual-mw-renewal-thread`). "Sure! Here's a reply:\n\n" is
  removed in `qual-jp-slack-thanks-nofact`.
- **Postamble removal:** "Let me know if you'd like me to adjust the tone or add more detail." is
  removed after the sign-off (`qual-mw-payment`).
- **Surrounding quotes:** removed in `qual-sb-li-congrats-nofact`.
- **Duplicate greeting:** removed both as a separate line ("Dear Tom,\n\n" when the field already
  has it, `qual-mw-greeting-typed`) and inline ("Hi Tomás! Great question!…" becomes "Great
  question!…" after a typed "Hi Tomás! ", `qual-sb-li-greeting-typed`).
- **Duplicate sign-off:** with the caret above an existing "Kind regards,\nMarcus\n<signature>",
  the draft's own "Kind regards,\nMarcus" is dropped and the inserted field reads correctly
  (`qual-mw-signoff-below`).
- **Bold:** `**…**` is stripped in plain-text email (`qual-mw-qbr-time`).
- **Selection insertion:** only the selection is replaced: "heads up: prod pg16 upgrade is nov
  14, 06:00-07:30 utc (90 min window). ping me w/ questions".
- **Draft/final superseded marking:** correct in all 3 draft-to-final pairs:
  - EUR 48,500 is marked "superseded by F1, its values are out of date".
  - Postgres on 7 Nov is marked superseded by 14 Nov.
  - Offer 172,000 / 1 Dec is marked superseded by 178,000 / 4 Jan.

  Facts are listed newest first, with "written <date>" labels.
- **Sensitive items stay out of the prompt:** none of the 6 sensitive items reached any prompt
  (checked for the treasury password, the surgery note, the pregnancy note, the SSN and the
  staging password). Each was excluded, as `local.include_sensitive=false` intends.
- **Per-recipient voice works:**
  - Elena gets "Good afternoon {first_name}," / "Best regards", and Priya gets "Dear
    {first_name}," / "Kind regards", with exemplars written to that same person.
  - Jun's Slack voice is "about 9 words", "casual", "Often starts messages in lowercase", with
    lowercase exemplars.
  - Sofia's LinkedIn voice shows emoji 0.3 and exclamation marks 1.7 per message, and her email
    voice (no emoji) shows "Warmly, {my_name}".
- **Prompt hygiene:** untrusted text is fenced (author `<…>` becomes `‹…›`). Thread messages
  carry `from="user"` / `from="other"` and timestamps. The prompt notes "(earlier messages
  omitted)" when it drops messages. The instruction ("decline politely") arrives in
  `<instruction>`.
- **Latency:** the scripted path has first-character p50 of 11 ms. All 31 drafts ran without a
  read-ahead, and prefix warms happened (`gg1 draft: done (… prefix warms)`).
