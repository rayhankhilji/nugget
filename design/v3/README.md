# v3 concept mockups (not applied to the app)

These follow the founder's v2 feedback: they had to look more modern and higher quality, and the
colours and name were wrong. The brand is therefore a placeholder:
- a neutral mark (a glass tile holding an accent dot) and a dashed wordmark slot;
- a single `--accent` token that carries all the brand colour. It defaults to macOS system blue,
  and the app can also follow the user's system accent through CSS `AccentColor`.

A new name, logo and colour drop into those two slots later.

All frames are real HTML and CSS, generated by `src/v3.mjs` and captured at 1280×800.

## Craft

- **Native window.**
  - The window has 12 px corners, a 0.5 px outer line and a 1 px inner highlight, and is lifted off
    the desktop by two shadow layers.
  - The traffic lights sit at 18 px with 8 px gaps, in a 52 px unified toolbar.
  - The sidebar uses vibrancy (`backdrop-filter: blur(40px) saturate(1.8)` over the wallpaper).
    Tauri's macOS `windowEffects: sidebar` gives the same result natively.
- **Type.** Inter (OFL) with its optical-size axis, so display sizes tighten automatically.
  - The UI base is 13 px with 1.38 line height. The scale is 11, 12, 13, 15, 17, 22, 28 and 40, and
    tracking tightens as sizes grow.
  - Source Serif 4 (OFL) is kept only for brief and draft prose.
  - Arabic, Devanagari and CJK fall back to Plex, Noto or the system fonts, as in v2.
- **Depth.**
  - Three shadow tokens (`--sh-1` for controls, `--sh-2` for cards and composer, `--sh-3` for
    popovers), each pairing a 0.5 px edge with soft blur.
  - Buttons get a 1 px top highlight and a gentle top-to-bottom gradient.
  - Hairlines are 0.5 px. Spacing is a 4-px grid: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 48.
- **States.** These are shown:
  - hover (a suggestion, a list row, a Connect button);
  - selected (accent-soft row);
  - focus (a 1 px accent ring plus a 3 px halo);
  - pressed (scale 0.97 and darker);
  - sending (the send button turns into a stop square with a pulse ring);
  - loading (skeletons with a sweep);
  - empty (concentric rings in the success colour plus three keyboard next-steps; no illustration).
- **Keyboard hints.** Raycast-style keycaps: a small gradient, a 0.5 px edge and a bottom lip.
  Every primary action shows its shortcut.
- **Logos.** The app logos come from simple-icons (CC0). Slack, Outlook and Microsoft Teams are
  missing because simple-icons removed them at the owners' request, so no frame draws them. The
  shipped app takes every logo from Composio's catalogue.

## Ghost text (replaces the Double Tap panel)

- **Ghost.** Text at about 37% opacity (38% in dark mode), drawn at the caret in the host app's own
  font size, starting directly after it. There's no box and no panel.
- **Streaming.** While text streams in, only the last few words shimmer, as an accent gradient
  fading to transparent. When the stream ends, the shimmer stops.
- **Hint chip.** One small 20 px vibrancy chip at the end of the suggestion: a dot in the accent
  colour (the brand slot) and the key to press. During a partial accept it reads "⌥→ word · Tab
  all". Keep typing to dismiss the suggestion, or press Esc.
- **Partial accept.** ⌥→ accepts one word, and Tab accepts the rest. Accepted words get a soft
  accent wash with a 1.5 px underline, which fades over 600 ms.
- **Accepted.** The chip turns into "2 sources". Clicking it, or pressing ⌥S, opens a mini sources
  popover with app logos and quoted snippets, plus "⌘Z undo".
- **Motion.** The ghost appears in 120 ms (opacity), and words stream at reading pace. The chip
  fades in 80 ms after the first word. Nothing captures input except Tab and ⌥→, and only while a
  suggestion is visible. With reduced motion there's no shimmer, and text appears in chunks.

## Rebuilding the frames

1. Install the fonts and icons: `cd src/fonts && npm i @fontsource-variable/inter
   @fontsource-variable/source-serif-4 simple-icons`. The frames use `simple-icons@16`.
2. From `src`, run `node v3.mjs $PWD/../out && node shoot.mjs $PWD/../out`.
